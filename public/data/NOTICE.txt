Atlante Deep Sky — data provenance

Stellarium DSO catalog version 3.23 standard:
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/master/nebulae/default/catalog.txt
Copyright Stellarium contributors. Distributed under GNU GPL version 2 or later,
following the Stellarium repository license. See COPYING-Stellarium.txt.
The unmodified source TSV is included as stellarium-catalog-source.tsv.
The derived selection, Italian labels and unit conversions in catalog.json are
also provided under GPL-2.0-or-later. Transformation source: build-catalog.py.
Coordinates and morphology are retained from the source; distances in kpc are
converted to light-years (1 kpc = 3261.56 light-years). Unknown values are null.
Selection: all Messier and Caldwell entries, plus named NGC/IC/Sh2/Barnard targets.
Caldwell 14 has two component records; catalogue numbers must not be counted
as one number per row.

Distance supplements (NASA/Hubble):
